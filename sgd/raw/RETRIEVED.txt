S1  BIS EFFECTIVE EXCHANGE RATES (WS_EER), MONTHLY, NOMINAL, BROAD BASKET
===============================================================================

Dataflow BIS,WS_EER,1.0. Series key FREQ.TYPE.BASKET.REF_AREA:
M = monthly, N = nominal, B = broad basket (64 economies), then the ISO
two-letter area code (euro area XM). Index 2020 = 100; geometric
trade-weighted average of bilateral rates; weights from manufacturing trade
with double weighting for third-market competition; weights time-varying on
three-year periods; broad monthly series from 1994 (BIS pages, UNVERIFIED
until the file is in). A rise means the currency strengthened against its
basket.

Series needed (label as shown in the index, "Nominal effective exchange
rate, <area> / Broad basket"):

  key        area            role                         label seen in index
  M.N.B.SG   Singapore       the piece                    yes
  M.N.B.JP   Japan           T2, T4, world view (float)   yes
  M.N.B.MY   Malaysia        T3, T4                       yes
  M.N.B.KR   Korea           T4, B                        yes
  M.N.B.CN   China           T4, B                        real (R) seen; N by pattern
  M.N.B.TH   Thailand        T4, B                        not seen; by pattern
  M.N.B.ID   Indonesia       T4, B                        yes
  M.N.B.US   United States   T4, B                        real (R) seen; N by pattern
  M.N.B.XM   Euro area       T4, B                        yes
  M.N.B.AU   Australia       T4, B                        not seen; by pattern
