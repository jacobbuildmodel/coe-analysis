# Feedback form text

Text for the three boxes of LTA's feedback form (go.gov.sg/coe-pcd-feedback). Every number
is from `out/facts.csv` and is checked by `14_manifest.py --check`, as in NOTE.md. Windows:
since January 2010, and since May 2022, when Category A's power threshold for electric cars
changed. Data to October 2026, exercise 1.

## Box 1. Merging Categories A and B with a feebate (consultation question 1)

From the published bidding record, Category A cleared above Category B in 3 of the 10 exercises from February to June 2026, consistent with paragraph 1.8, and in 21 of 397 exercises since January 2010. The median premium gap (B minus A) was $9,010 since January 2010 and $17,999 since May 2022 (16 and 18 per cent of Category A's premium), beside the paper's example band steps of $7,500 and $15,000. Against the same exercise 12 months earlier, the gap changed by a median of $6,603 since January 2010 and $9,512 since May 2022. The renewal-price (PQP) gap was $2,901 in October 2026 (2.3 per cent of Category A's PQP); its median since May 2022 was $19,254. Full note, data and code: https://github.com/jacobbuildmodel/coe-analysis (folder lta-ab).

## Box 2. Category E (consultation question 2)

Category E received 15 per cent of the combined Category A, B and E quota since January 2010 and 11 per cent since May 2022. Since May 2022 it cleared at or above both Categories A and B in 74 of 107 exercises and below Category B in 33; its median premium was $888 above Category B's. The data do not show which vehicles were registered with Category E certificates (paragraph 2.22).
